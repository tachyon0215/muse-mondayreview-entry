# muse-mondayreview-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Monday, Reviewed in One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zeroxfive-monday-reviewed-in-one-line-e55de3c1).

## The entry

> One star: Monday checked in at 6 a.m. with no reservation, took my whole day as a deposit, and left a meeting in my inbox.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: A true one-line review with hospitality specifics (no reservation, deposit, something left behind) mapped onto Monday, clear on a first read and tight to the last word.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 cc04d45a5d1263bf9c13ca501b85d3a67d66f296422c3bad531b97ffa6e2ce00).
